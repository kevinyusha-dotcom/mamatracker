ママトラカ Setup

1 · Connect to the bus

Uses the parent login from the Cpark GPS360 app. Tap Sign in with Cpark, log in, and the key fills in. If no window opens, sign in, then paste the access_token value from the address bar here.

2 · Stops

Paste a Google Maps link, coordinates (long-press the spot in Google Maps and copy the numbers) or an address. Or tap Place on map.

3 · Which alerts

Each alert fires once per morning and once per afternoon, however the bus loops around.

4 · Arrival time & traffic

Without a Google key, ママトラカ uses the real road route (OpenStreetMap) and adds your traffic factor. With a Google Maps key it uses Google's live-traffic time, the same number Google Maps shows.

5 · Phone push (optional)

Install the free ntfy app, subscribe to the same topic, and alerts arrive even with the phone locked (while ママトラカ runs on one device). Pick a hard-to-guess name.
